About Us

Ku'upua Wong is 12 years old and is a rising star in both volleyball and basketball. She has been playing basketball since she was 4 years old and started playing volleyball at 9 years old. She has played for different teams and clubs over the years and is currently playing basketball for Swish and a travel team called Blue Wave. Ku'upua is also currently playing for Pana'ewa Volleyball Club.

Kamana'o Wong is 10 years old and is also a rising star in both baseball and basketball. He has been playing basketball since he was 6 years old and started playing baseball at 7 years old. He has played for different teams over the years and is currently playing baseball for Hilo Ball Stars and two travel teams called Mynah Byrdz and Hawaii Woodbat Academy. Kamana'o is also playing basketball for Swish and Hilo Hoopers.

Ku'upua and Kamana'o are both natural born athletes with the potential to play on a much bigger platform.
