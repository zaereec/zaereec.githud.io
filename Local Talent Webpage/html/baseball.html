Kamana'o's Baseball Journey

Kamana'o Wong started playing baseball at 7 years old for a team called Cuzins. He immediately played in the coach pitch division and by the end of the season became one of the star athletes on the team. During his second season he played in both coach pitch and little league practicing five days a week and playing games every weekend. In his third season, Kamana'o played with Keaukaha Cuzins 10U baseball team, Waiakea Uka Tigers 14U baseball team and had the opportunity to play travel ball with Hawaii Woodbat Academy.

Kamana'o was fortunate to travel to Las Vegas to play in the Cranberry Classic Tournament. He made a name for himself, hitting homeruns in every single game he played that weekend. As much as possible, Kamana'o looks forward to more travel opportunities in the future.
