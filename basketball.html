Ku'upua and Kamana'o's Basketball Journey

Ku'upua and Kamana'o started playing basketball at a young age. Ku'upua was able to start playing at just 4 years old. Kamana'o couldn't join the team until he was 6 years old but had to play up in the 8U division. Both Ku'upua and Kamana'o started playing for a team called B-Elite. Ku'upua was able to play two seasons before COVID-19 shut everything down. Kamana'o was able to join the team when things started to re-open after COVID.

After 3 seasons with B-Elite, Ku'upua switched to an all-girls year around basketball team called SWISH, whom she is still currently playing for. Kamana'o was able to join SWISH after Ku'upua had been playing there for almost two years as the coach decided to become a co-ed organization. Kamana'o is also still currently playing with SWISH as well.

Earlier this year, Ku'upua was introduced to an Oahu team called Blue Wave whom she impressed so much after playing one tournament that they asked her to travel with them to California for the Girls Jr. Nationals Basketball Tournament. She has just been invited to travel with Blue Wave again to the Adidas Girls Jr. National Basketball Tournament in California in May 2027.

Also, earlier this year, Kamana'o was invited to join another local basketball team called Hilo Hoopers. Alongside playing with SWISH, he is also currently playing with Hilo Hoopers and although was unable to travel to Kaua'i with them this year, hopefully he will be invited and able to travel with them in the future.
