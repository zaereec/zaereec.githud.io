Ku'upua's Volleyball Journey

Ku'upua Wong started playing volleyball at 9 nine years old. The first volleyball club that she played for was High Intensity. She was the youngest player on the team with girls who had already been playing volleyball competitively for 2-3 years. Since the team was going to be traveling for the summer, the coach decided to train her alongside her granddaughter and later introduced her to her new coach.

Ku'upua played her first volleyball club season with 'Eleu Malulani Volleyball Club. The next year she decided to move on to a new team who she had been training with over the next summer, Lawakua Volleyball Club. And now she is playing with Pana'ewa Volleyball Club.
